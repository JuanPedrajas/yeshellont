Yes Hello, Boss!

nohello.net asks you not to send a lone "hello" and then wait.

It does not say that a reply must arrive in under one second.

It does not say that a link is a substitute for a sentence.

It is ok to be a human being who was making coffee, in a meeting, or thinking.

The principle is "put the question in the first message." Nobody wrote a clause about latency SLAs. If we had, they would have been in the footnotes, with a pager rotation.

Sending nohello.net to someone for taking 1.2 seconds is the most "hello, are you there?" thing you can do. It is a hello with extra steps.
